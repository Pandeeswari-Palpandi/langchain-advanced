### 1. **Rava Upma with Mixed Vegetables**
- **Ingredients**: Rava (semolina), mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans), mustard seeds, urad dal, green chilies, ginger, and curry leaves.
- **Preparation**: Roast the rava until golden and set aside. In a pan, heat oil, add mustard seeds and let them splutter. Add urad dal, chopped green chilies, ginger, curry leaves, and mixed vegetables. Sauté for a few minutes, then add water and bring it to a boil. Stir in the roasted rava, cook until the water is absorbed, and serve hot with coconut chutney.

### 2. **Quinoa Idli with Coconut Chutney**
- **Ingredients**: Quinoa, urad dal, ginger, green chilies, and salt.
- **Preparation**: Soak quinoa and urad dal overnight. Grind them together into a smooth batter, adding salt and chopped ginger and green chilies. Pour into idli molds and steam for about 10-12 minutes. Serve with freshly made coconut chutney.

### 3. **Vegetable Dosa with Spinach and Lentils**
- **Ingredients**: Brown rice, urad dal, spinach, and mixed vegetables (like carrots and bell peppers).
- **Preparation**: Soak brown rice and urad dal overnight, then grind them into a batter. Add finely chopped spinach and mixed vegetables to the batter. Heat a tawa and pour a ladle of the batter, spreading it into a thin dosa. Cook until crisp and serve with tomato chutney.

### 4. **Oats Porridge with Banana and Nut Butter**
- **Ingredients**: Rolled oats, milk (or a plant-based alternative), banana, and nut butter (like peanut or almond).
- **Preparation**: Cook rolled oats in milk until creamy. Top with sliced banana and a spoonful of nut butter for added flavor and protein. Optionally sprinkle with some cinnamon or chia seeds.

### 5. **Millet Upma with Curry Leaves and Peanuts**
- **Ingredients**: Foxtail millet (or any local millet), peanuts, mustard seeds, urad dal, green chilies, onions, and curry leaves.
- **Preparation**: Rinse and cook the millet in water until fluffy. In a pan, heat oil, add mustard seeds, urad dal, chopped onions, green chilies, and curry leaves. Add peanuts and sauté until golden. Mix in the cooked millet and season with salt. Serve warm.

### 2. **Ragi Puttu with Coconut and Banana**
   - **Ingredients:** Ragi flour, grated coconut, bananas, salt, and water.
   - **Preparation:** 
     1. Mix ragi flour with a pinch of salt and water to form a crumbly mixture.
     2. Layer the mixture with grated coconut in a puttu maker.
     3. Steam for about 10-15 minutes.
     4. Serve hot with sliced bananas and a drizzle of honey or jaggery syrup.

### 3. **Vegetable Dosa with Spinach and Methi**
   - **Ingredients:** Dosa batter, spinach, fenugreek leaves, onions, and green chilies.
   - **Preparation:** 
     1. Mix finely chopped spinach and methi leaves with the dosa batter.
     2. Add chopped onions and green chilies for flavor.
     3. Heat a pan and pour a ladle of the batter, spreading it into a thin dosa.
     4. Cook until crispy and serve with coconut chutney.

### 4. **Chickpea Sundal**
   - **Ingredients:** Boiled chickpeas, grated coconut, mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chilies, and salt.
   - **Preparation:**
     1. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds and curry leaves.
     2. Add chopped green chilies and the boiled chickpeas, sautéing for a few minutes.
     3. Mix in grated coconut and salt, cooking for an additional minute.
     4. Serve warm as a protein-rich breakfast.

### 5. **Oats Idli with Sambar**
   - **Ingredients:** Oats, fermented idli batter, grated carrots, and spices for flavor.
   - **Preparation:**
     1. Grind oats into a fine powder and mix with fermented idli batter.
     2. Add grated carrots and spices (like cumin and pepper) to the mixture.
     3. Pour into idli molds and steam for about 10-12 minutes.
     4. Serve hot with sambar and chutney for a nutritious start to the day.
